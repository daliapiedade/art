### Notes
* The pattern calls German Short Rows.
* I've adapted the pattern to be knitted in portuguese style. This way, the yarn is worked in front work, making it easier and quicker to work the purls instead of the knits. 
* Since the length of the short rows varies a lot, I decided to create a memory palace to keep track of the turning points.
