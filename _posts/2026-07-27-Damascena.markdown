### Notes
* The pattern calls German Short Rows.
* Since the length of the short rows varies a lot, I decided to create a memory palace to keep track of the turning points.
* I've adapted the pattern to be knitted in portuguese style. 
 * This way the yarn is worked in front work, making it easier and quicker to work the purls instead of the knits. 
 * To tension the yarn, I use a hair claw to tension tjhe working yarn. It's not practical - and i don't like - to wrap it around the neck and I don't like to use the knitting pin because it can damage (making holes) the clothes i'm wearing.
